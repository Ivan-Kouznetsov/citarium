APA 7th Edition Quick Reference Guide

1. Journal Articles:

Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of article in sentence case. Title of Periodical in Title Case, volume(issue), pages. https://doi.org/xxxx

2. Books:

Author, A. A. (Year). Title of work in sentence case (edition). Publisher. https://doi.org/xxxx

3. Book Chapters:

Author, A. A. (Year). Title of chapter. In E. E. Editor (Ed.), Title of book (pp. xx-xx). Publisher.

4. In-Text Citations:

• 1 Author: (Smith, 2020) or Smith (2020)
• 2 Authors: (Smith & Jones, 2020) or Smith and Jones (2020)
• 3+ Authors: (Smith et al., 2020) or Smith et al. (2020)
